The theme is resonance

Resonance is the invisible thread, the subtle yet profound force that binds existence together.

The Nature

Resonance can be felt in the stillness before rain, as nature holds its breath. It is there in the first drop that sends a shiver through your skin; in the rhythm of tides, the turning of seasons, and the quiet dialogue between all living things.

The Human

Resonance is felt when a stranger's laughter finds its way to your smile, or when another person's tragedy makes your heart heavy and your eyes tear up. It lives in love and longing, grief and remembrance, and the unspoken understanding between strangers. Resonance is found where one life leaves its impact on another.

The Collective

Resonance is the force that turns a whisper into a chant, a lone voice into a chorus, a small act of resistance into a revolution, and a shared belief into a movement. It transforms a simple act of kindness into a wave that travels far beyond its origin. It echoes through communities, traditions, celebrations, protests, and moments of shared hope, reminding us that the greatest changes often begin with the smallest gestures.

The Journey

Resonance lives in forgotten streets, inherited traditions, weathered photographs, and familiar faces. It reminds us that every journey, across landscapes, cultures, or through time itself, changes not only where we arrive, but also who we become.

The Vision

Resonance is not only found in what we photograph, but in how we choose to see. It emerges through light and shadow, composition and timing, perspective and intention. Every frame is an interpretation. Every photograph is a quiet invitation to see the world as we do, and to resonate with it.

We believe resonance goes far beyond sound. For this exhibition, we invite you to explore what this concept means to you, whether through a single defining image or a complete photo story. Let these themes be your starting point, not your boundary. We are looking for photographs that don't just document resonance, but actively create it.

Eligibility

Voyage of Visuals: Resonance is open to amateur and professional photographers from anywhere in the world. Only online entries are eligible.

Submission period

Submissions are open now and close at 11:59 pm on 7 August. Late entries will not be accepted.

Judging & results

Judges will be announced soon. The selection list will be published on our Facebook and Instagram pages, and selected participants will also be contacted personally.

Registration & prizes

Selected photographers will be contacted about registration after the results are published. Prize details will be announced on the event page.
