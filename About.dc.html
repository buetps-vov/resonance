Our History

In 1997, a handful of photography enthusiasts found each other in the corridors of BUET and decided their shared obsession deserved a home. That home became the BUET Photographic Society. What began as informal adda over cameras and contact sheets has grown into one of the largest and most prolific university based photography clubs in Bangladesh.

Through workshops, photowalks, screenings and exhibitions, BUETPS has spent nearly three decades helping its members sharpen their eyes and find their voices. Our photographers have carried the society's spirit far beyond campus, earning recognition at home and abroad.

In 2023 we marked our 25th anniversary with Remembrance, an international exhibition that looked back at the road travelled. Voyage of Visuals continues that journey, an annual stage where the next chapter gets written, one photograph at a time.
